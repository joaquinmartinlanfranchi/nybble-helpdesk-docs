Known Limits of V1

LimitWhat it means functionallyStatus
No operator ↔ user chatThe conversation after escalation happens over Slack or email. The definition of "Resolved" that says "the agent helps them directly via chat" doesn't reflect the app. LABS-516 LABS-547out of V1 per the 8/19 decision
"Returned" is a dead endThe user sees the request but has no way to act on it or reply. Moving it back to In Review would be done manually by the operator, but that manual change is not implemented today. LABS-547known
The bot doesn't read imagesAttachments are possible (only via +, only after the first response) but the bot doesn't interpret the content, not even screenshots of material that's in the KB. LABS-515Re-open
The operator can't attach anythingOnly "Return" opens a text box, and without attachments. No way to leave evidence. LABS-516Re-open
No notificationsNo status change notifies the user. The email preference existed, didn't work, and the panel was hidden. LABS-552 LABS-511doesn't exist
No SLANo clocks, thresholds, or time-based alerts. Explicit decision.not planned
Times entered manuallyEvery time-based KPI depends on the operator filling in the field correctly. There's no automatic measurement.by design
The quality score doesn't cover the main chatThe highest-volume feature has no LLM-judge-style quality score. LABS-529Re-open
