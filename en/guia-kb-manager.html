Nybble AI Help Desk · KB Manager Guide

Quick Guide · KB Manager kb

How to upload and maintain the Knowledge Base the bot uses to answer. Meant to stay open in one tab while you work in the platform on the other — no need to read it end to end before getting started.

Why what you upload here matters The bot only answers with what it finds in the Knowledge Base — it never makes up an answer or fills in gaps with general knowledge. If a document isn't loaded, or is out of date, that query will end up escalated to a human operator. Clear, current documentation is what makes the desk assertive.

Step by Step

Step 1 · Enter the Knowledge Base kb
  1. Log in with your KB Manager user.
  2. From the menu, go to the Knowledge Base section.
  3. There you'll see the list of everything already loaded, with its indexing status per document.

If this section doesn't show up in your menu, your user doesn't have the KB Manager role assigned — ask the Tenant Admin to enable it for you.

Step 2 · Upload a document kb
  1. From the Knowledge Base, find the option to upload a new document and select the file.
  2. Recommended format: .doc / .docx / PDF for now.
  3. Upload happens one document at a time. Repeat the step for each file.
  4. After uploading, the desk triggers indexing on its own — no additional action needed on your part.
Step 3 · Set the scope kb
  1. When uploading, you can assign the document to one or more Companies in the tenant.
  2. If you don't assign any, the document stays tenant-wide: available to all Companies in the tenant.
  3. If you assign it to one or more Companies, the bot will only use it to answer queries from those specific Companies.

Use this to separate content that shouldn't cross between clients or areas — for example, internal policies specific to one Company.

Step 4 · Confirm it indexed correctly kb
  1. Every document goes through: Pending → Indexing → Ready (or Error).
  2. A newly uploaded document isn't available to the bot until it reaches "Ready".
  3. If it ends up in Error: it won't be used to answer. Check the file format and try the upload again.

The KB topic summary (what the bot sees to know what's out of scope) regenerates on its own every time you upload, sync, or a document's status changes — you don't need to do anything manually for that.

Step 5 · Keep it current kb
  • Clear, current content: the bot answers literally with what's in there, so an ambiguous document produces an ambiguous answer.
  • Avoid contradictory content between two documents on the same topic — the bot has no way to choose which one is correct.
  • Did a policy or process change? Re-upload the updated version; there's no inline editing of content already loaded.
  • Check every so often that no documents are left unresolved in Error status.

Before You Upload, Keep This in Mind

Known issues during the pilot
  • Loading the KB from external sources (Google Drive) is not yet defined for the pilot. LABS-576

Quick Checklist

Do This
  • Upload as .doc / .docx / PDF
  • One document per topic, no contradictions between files
  • Verify that the document reached Ready status
  • Keep your own copy of every file you upload
Avoid This
  • Assuming a document is loaded when it's stuck in Error
