Nybble AI Help Desk · User Guide

Quick Guide · User usuario

How to use the support chat, what to expect when the AI can't resolve your issue, and where to track the status of your query. Meant to stay open while you use the help desk, not to be read end to end before you start.

The first thing to know The bot only answers with what it finds in your company's Knowledge Base. If it doesn't find anything relevant in 3 attempts, if it detects a critical keyword, or if you explicitly ask to speak with a person, your query is automatically escalated to a human operator — and the chat closes at that same moment.

Step by Step

Step 1 · Ask your question usuario
  1. Open the chat and sign in with your user account.
  2. Write your question with as much detail as possible — the more specific it is, the better the bot's search through the Knowledge Base.
  3. You can attach a file with the + button, but only after the bot responds to your first message.
Step 2 · Confirm whether it helped usuario
  1. After the bot's first response, a red button appears to end the conversation.
  2. When you close it, you'll be asked "Did this answer help you?" with Yes / No buttons.
  3. If you answer Yes, your query is marked Resolved by AI and requires no further action from you.
  4. If you don't answer, after 45 minutes the system assumes it did help and closes the conversation anyway.
Step 3 · If the bot can't resolve it usuario
  1. You can explicitly ask to speak with a human at any point — it escalates immediately.
  2. If the bot fails 3 times searching the Knowledge Base, it escalates on its own.
  3. In either case, the chat closes automatically and it does not ask whether it was helpful — an escalation is already a closure in itself.
  4. Your query moves to Pending in an operator's queue.
Step 4 · Track your case in My Queries usuario
  1. Go to My Queries to see the status: Pending, In Review, Resolved, or Resolved by AI.
  2. There you'll see only your own queries, never those of other users.
  3. You can filter by status.
Step 5 · If an operator returns it to you usuario
  • The operator can mark your incident as Returned when they need something from you to continue (a piece of information, an additional step).
  • Today there's no way to respond from the platform to a Returned incident — the operator will reach out to you via Slack or email, outside the help desk.
  • Once you respond through that channel, the operator changes the status manually.

Things to Keep in Mind

Known Issues During the Pilot
  • If your incident ends up Returned, you have no way to act on it or continue the conversation within the platform — this is the most significant functional gap in the product today. LABS-547
  • There are no notifications. No status change notifies you by email — you have to go into My Queries to find out. LABS-552 LABS-511
  • The bot doesn't interpret images, not even screenshots of something already in the Knowledge Base. LABS-515

Quick Checklist

Do This
  • Be specific in your first question
  • Answer Yes / No to "did this help?" so it's classified correctly
  • Check My Queries if you haven't gotten a response — you won't receive an email
Avoid This
  • Expecting the operator to write to you inside the platform after a Returned status
  • Pasting an image directly into the chat — today it can only be attached with the + button
